## 🎮 About the Game

Crimson Moon is a gothic action-adventure RPG developed by ProbablyMonsters and released on September 1, 2026. Players control a Nephilim, a human-angel hybrid fighting demons, undead, vampires, and Hellgrowth abominations in the cursed city of Gildenarch.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Crimson Moon**. This trainer enhances Crimson Moon's solo campaign by providing control over player survivability, combat damage, enemy resistance, and equipment progression. It is designed around the game's melee combat, supernatural powers, missions, weapon and armor upgrades, and boss encounters.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more accessible solo experience, faster equipment testing, easier boss fights, or freedom to explore Gildenarch without the standard combat difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the game's normal movement and traversal controls. The trainer does not replace movement inputs, so maintain control of the Nephilim while activating defensive or combat options when entering dangerous areas.

### Tools & Inventory

Equip weapons and armor through the game's normal inventory and build systems. Activate equipment-related options only after the desired item is equipped, then verify the result before changing loadouts or visiting the Blacksmith.

### World & Environment

Missions are selected through the Sanctus Clypeus and its War Table as you work to reclaim districts of Gildenarch and push back Hellgrowth. Use the trainer to support exploration and mission completion, but disable combat options before scripted transitions or major progression events.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate survivability options before engaging large groups or bosses. Damage enhancements are most useful against demons, undead, vampires, Infernal Legion forces, and Dead Gods. Disable one-hit and damage modifiers when testing normal parries, finishers, angelic arts, or transformations.
